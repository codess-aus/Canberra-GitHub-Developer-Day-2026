    Know which agents exist and what they do; contain their access; protect the data and tools in their path; adapt controls as risk changes.

## Treat access as an operational responsibility

Agents can read context, call tools and make changes on a person's behalf. A governance model should identify the agent and its owner, restrict access to what the task requires, protect connected systems and review activity as behaviour or risk changes.

GitHub's AI controls and agent control plane provide policy and audit capabilities. Enterprise MCP allowlisting and scanning for AI applications are shown as preview features. These are useful layers, but they do not eliminate the need to review permissions, untrusted inputs and the approval process for consequential actions.
