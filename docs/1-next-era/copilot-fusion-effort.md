## Set the effort for the work

The slide separates the decision a developer makes from the one Fusion makes. Developers choose **Standard**, **High** or **Max** to express how much effort a task warrants. Fusion then chooses an execution pattern: one model can solve the task directly, a cascade can escalate a draft when needed, or a critique pattern can use an independent model to review and help revise the draft.

For routine work, Standard is the lower-friction choice. High and Max are aimed at harder problems where deeper reasoning may be useful. More effort can involve additional model work and AI credit usage, so choose it when the task benefits from that extra analysis rather than treating Max as the default for everything.

Organisations retain policy control over which models Fusion can use. The [Fusion overview](copilot-fusion.md) covers effort levels and billing in more detail.
