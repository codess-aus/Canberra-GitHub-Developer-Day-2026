## Choose the task, not a provider

The image presents Copilot Fusion as a layer above individual models. Its job is to assess what a task needs, then choose a suitable model or coordinate more than one. The goal is to reduce the need for developers to predict which model will perform best for each kind of reasoning, code generation, debugging or tool use.

Fusion is an orchestrator, not a new foundation model. It operates within the models and policies available to an organisation. The developer can choose an effort level; Fusion selects the execution approach for the task.

The original [Copilot Fusion page](copilot-fusion.md) explains the execution patterns, local model support and administrator controls. The following pages focus on the benchmark shown in the keynote and the effort choices available to developers.
