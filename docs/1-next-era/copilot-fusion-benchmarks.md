## Read the comparison carefully

The chart plots coding-agent benchmark score against average cost per task. A higher score and lower cost are favourable, which is why the highlighted region sits toward the upper-right. GitHub reports that Copilot Fusion Max scored within two points of Claude Code with Opus 5.5 Max, at about half the cost and around 40% less time per task.

Those are results reported for a specific benchmark and configuration, not a guarantee about every repository or task. A benchmark can help compare approaches, but real work also depends on the codebase, instructions, tools, evaluation criteria and how much review is required. Test representative tasks from your own workflow before drawing a budget or quality conclusion.

For the mechanism behind the comparison, see [how Copilot Fusion coordinates models](copilot-fusion-models.md) and the existing [Fusion overview](copilot-fusion.md).
