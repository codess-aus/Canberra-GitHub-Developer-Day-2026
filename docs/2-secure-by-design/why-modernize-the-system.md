**Section 01**

## Why Modernize the System

Building trust through goals and evidence.

## Talk notes

By now, we've all experimented with AI agents in some capacity. And chances are, we've also hit the same walls: integration friction, scaling limits, low production rates, and security roadblocks. It seems like our current approach won't hold up in our work environments, and we also fear messing it up.

So, our starting question wasn't just whether agents could write a feature. It was whether we could build a repeatable system that would reliably deliver production-quality software with AI. In order to trust a system, we have to see clear goals, guardrails, and evidence, and we also have to see these put into practice in the real world.

So, to put it to the test, we used the Microsoft 1 Engineering System as our proving ground.
