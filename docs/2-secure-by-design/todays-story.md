    **From a trusted engineering loop to a connected product system**

    1. **Why Modernize:** Building trust through goals and evidence
    2. **Microsoft 1ES Modernization outcome:** Turn intent into validated code through an agentic SDLC
    3. **Modernization Demo:** Connect PM, Design, Data Science, and Engineering
    4. **Key Takeaways:** Build on our learnings. Adapt them to your needs.

The talk has four parts. The slide frames the journey as a move **from a trusted engineering loop to a connected product system**.

## 1. Why modernise the system

Trust comes from goals and evidence. [Why modernize the system](why-modernize-the-system.md) asks what it takes to trust agents with production-quality software, and answers with three things you can see: goals, guardrails and evidence.

## 2. How Microsoft builds 1ES to modernise development practice

The 1ES outcome is to turn intent into validated code through an agentic software development life cycle (SDLC). The results are on [AI assistants across the engineering system](ai-assistants-across-the-engineering-system.md) and [GitHub Copilot @ Microsoft highlights](github-copilot-at-microsoft.md).

## 3. A demo of GitHub Copilot modernization

The slide describes the demo as connecting PM, design, data science and engineering. On the tooling side, Microsoft Learn describes a three-stage workflow of assessment, planning and execution. The agent writes its plan as Markdown files under `.github/upgrades` in your repository, so the plan lives alongside the code it describes.

## 4. What changed in how we work

The close combines the [Takeaways](takeaways.md) with [What changed in how we work](what-changed-in-how-we-work.md): build on what Microsoft learned, then adapt it to your own needs.

## What to listen for

Three threads run through all four parts: clear goals, guardrails that hold when the agent is wrong, and evidence that lets you decide how much autonomy to grant.
