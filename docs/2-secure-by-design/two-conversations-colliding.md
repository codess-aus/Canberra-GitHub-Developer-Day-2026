**Context**

## Near term: Managing legacy risk under a ticking clock

- Legacy estates with accumulating weaknesses
- Developer productivity under pressure
- Strict PSPF 002 compliance and remediation mandates

## Paradigm shift: AI is transforming how software is built

- Planning, quality, security, and ops must evolve too
- Faster code output alone does not yield better outcomes
- Modernisation needs a repeatable engineering system

**PSPF 002:** Legacy stopped being a someday problem. Keeping vulnerable systems running is no longer an acceptable risk.
