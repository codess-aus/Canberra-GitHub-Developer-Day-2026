## The frame for the rest of the keynote

This slide is the map. Its diagram, a line running through **Plan, Build, Review and Ship**, reappears at the bottom of every product slide that follows, with the relevant stages lit up. The [chapter landing page](index.md) has a table of which slide highlights which stage.

The phrase worth noticing is "the development experience organizations already rely on". The pitch is not a separate AI product to adopt alongside your tools. It is AI, collaboration and security arriving inside the places where your teams already plan, review and ship.

## Four themes, four product stories

1. **AI for complex work, with less toil.** Covered by [Copilot Projects](copilot-projects.md) and [Copilot Automations](copilot-automations.md).
2. **Faster, more reliable review and collaboration.** Both Projects and Automations touch review, and Automations can learn from how reviewers respond.
3. **Security closer to where development happens.** Covered by [AI Scan](ai-scan-for-repositories.md).
4. **Simpler access to Copilot, with the freedom to choose models and developer tools.** Covered by [Simpler access to AI](simpler-access-to-ai.md) and [Copilot Fusion](copilot-fusion.md).

## A new surface: the GitHub Copilot app

Much of what GitHub showed at Universe lives in the GitHub Copilot app. According to GitHub Docs, it is a desktop application purpose-built for agent-driven development. It gives you one place to direct agents across parallel workstreams, work with issues and pull requests, and manage the lifecycle without switching between terminals, IDEs and browser tabs.

The app became generally available for macOS, Windows and Linux on 17 June 2026. The Universe blog describes it as designed from the ground up for agentic software delivery, and says it is for more than producing code: you can write specs, run design discussions or analyse telemetry in the same agent-first surface.

For administrators, one point from the general availability announcement is worth checking: on Copilot Business or Copilot Enterprise plans, the changelog said an organisation or enterprise admin must have the Copilot CLI enabled in policy settings before people can use the app.
